## Breathe Barcelona Dashboard

The interactive dashboard translates the analytical workflow into five connected views, covering urban air-quality exploration, temporal and spatial modelling, model interpretability, and prediction.

### Home

### Overview

City-level overview of air quality, meteorological conditions and spatial NO₂ patterns.

### Temporal Analysis

Temporal evolution, COVID-period comparison, environmental and mobility drivers, and time-series forecasting.

### Spatial Analysis

Spatial distribution of NO₂, local spatial autocorrelation, cluster detection and spatial modelling at census-section level.

### Model Insights

Model performance, feature importance, SHAP explainability and Leave-One-Station-Out validation.

### Dual Predictor — Spatial

Interactive estimation of annual NO₂ concentration from urban and mobility characteristics.

### Dual Predictor — Temporal

Interactive classification of daily air-quality scenarios from environmental and mobility conditions.
